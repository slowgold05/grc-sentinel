# SOX Section 404 source review

Status: ICFR objective and candidate-evaluation foundation implemented; activation remains
protected pending securities-law and audit review.

## Authority

- Publisher: U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)
- Primary rule release: SEC Release No. 33-8238, *Management's Report on Internal Control Over
  Financial Reporting and Certification of Disclosure in Exchange Act Periodic Reports*
- Official source: https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/33-8238.htm
- Current SEC reporting reference: https://www.sec.gov/about/divisions-offices/division-corporation-finance/financial-reporting-manual/frm-topic-4

## Reviewed scope boundaries

- Section 404(a) is represented as an annual ICFR management-reporting objective for companies
  filing periodic reports under Exchange Act Section 13(a) or 15(d), not as a cybersecurity law.
- Registered investment companies, asset-backed issuers, private companies, and applicable newly
  public transition cases must remain distinguishable rather than collapsed into “not public.”
- SEC guidance states that non-accelerated filers and emerging growth companies do not include the
  auditor attestation required by Section 404(b). That does not by itself remove management's
  Section 404(a) assessment obligation.
- Filer category, reporting-period end, management-assessment requirement/result, and auditor-
  attestation requirement/result are therefore separate facts.

## Control and evidence boundary

IT general controls may support ICFR and the reliability of financial reporting, but SOX does not
supply a general cybersecurity control catalog. Any objective-to-control relationship must come
from an approved audit methodology or sourced crosswalk; semantic similarity is insufficient.

## Activation gate

A securities-law/audit reviewer must approve filer categories, exclusions, transition treatment,
citations, and positive/negative/unknown golden profiles. Evidence expectations and ITGC mappings
must retain their publisher and version. Ollama may draft text from an approved retrieval context;
it must not decide reporting status, attestation scope, or control equivalence.
