Repository review date: **2026-09-11**.

The second edition translates governance principles into organizational practices. The GenAI
framework adds nine ecosystem dimensions. The Agentic AI framework addresses risk bounding,
meaningful human accountability, lifecycle controls, and end-user responsibility for systems able
to reason and act through tools.

Model each edition as a separately versioned voluntary assurance objective. Traditional, GenAI,
and agentic scope must not be collapsed into a legal “Singapore AI compliance” result. Import only
publisher-provided content with provenance; preserve edition boundaries; do not infer crosswalks.
Any MAS FEAT, Veritas, or future supervisory overlay requires its own current MAS primary-source
review before it can be represented as finance-sector guidance or an executable obligation.

Acceptance case: the fictional RAG assistant may select the 2024 GenAI objective; the 2026 agentic
objective remains unselected while it has no tools or external-action authority.
