ISO describes the standard as specifying requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining,
and continually improving an AI management system. Its management-system approach uses continual
improvement rather than classifying an individual system as legally applicable.

Until the project has a licensed source, store only the identifier, edition, publisher, URL,
assurance-objective metadata, and other publicly permitted metadata. Do not copy clause or annex
text, reconstruct it from model recall, or claim certification. Clause/control ingestion and any
mapping require licensed input and separate human review.

voluntary standard”; it creates no legal determination and retrieves no unlicensed text.
