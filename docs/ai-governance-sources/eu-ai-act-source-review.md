The candidate intake must preserve Article 2 territorial/operator scope, Article 3 definitions,
the organization's operator role, intended purpose, prohibited-practice facts, Article 6 and Annex
I/III high-risk paths, general-purpose-model role, and Article 50 transparency scenarios. These are
separate branches; “uses AI” is not an applicability rule.

The European Commission records phased application and 2026 changes to high-risk-system dates.
Therefore, every decision must store the consolidated source version, evaluation date, applicable
transition date, and the role evaluated. Missing or ambiguous facts return `needs_review`.

No production ruleset may activate until EU counsel approves scope, definitions, exclusions,
prohibited/high-risk/transparency branches, dates, citations, and at least 30 boundary profiles.
Requirements must come from official EUR-Lex data; control mappings must be sourced, never inferred.
